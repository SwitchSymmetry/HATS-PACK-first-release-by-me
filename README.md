Welcome to the official user guide for the SwitchSymmetry HATS Pack! This pack is an all-in-one custom firmware (CFW) bundle containing everything you need to safely run and manage your Nintendo Switch. It has been fully tested and verified working on real console hardware!
📦 What's Included Atmosphere: The core custom firmware framework.Hekate: The powerful custom bootloader and configuration menu.DBI & DBI-Russian: The ultimate game, save, and file manager (includes Russian translation).emummc.txt: Pre-configured file to handle safe routing and help block Nintendo telemetry servers.
🛠️ Step-by-Step InstallationStep 1: Prepare Your SD Card Power off your Nintendo Switch completely.Remove the microSD card from your console and insert it into your PC.Important: Back up your current Nintendo folder and any existing emuMMC files to your computer just in case.
Step 2: Download and Extract the Pack Download the latest release archive from this repository.Extract (unzip) the contents of the pack directly onto your computer.
Step 3: Copy Files to Your SD CardCopy all the extracted folders and files (atmosphere, bootloader, emummc.txt, etc.) from the pack.Paste them directly into the root (the absolute main directory) of your microSD card.
Step 4: Boot into Custom Firmware (OLED Switch)Safely eject the microSD card from your PC and slide it back into your OLED Switch.Ensure your console is completely powered off.Press the Power Button once normally.Your internal modchip will automatically glitch the system and launch directly into the Hekate bootloader menu on your OLED screen.Tap Launch on the touchscreen and select your Custom Firmware profile (such as emuMMC) to boot into Atmosphere.
